How it works

Simple, on purpose.

1

Watch

Sellers host their shows live. You see the goods, the seller, and the giving — all before the first bid.

2

Bid fair

Rounds run on a fair-close clock. Late bids extend the round, so lots are won by wanting them, not by gaming the last second.

3

See the giving

Every lot states its giving up front, in plain words. When a sale closes, you know exactly what it did.

Why “altruistic”?

Most markets ask only, “what’s it worth?” We also ask, “who does it help?” Almus Mercatus is being built around generosity as a feature, not an afterthought.
